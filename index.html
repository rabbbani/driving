About PARKLITE

PARKLITE is a lightweight 3D manual driving and parking simulator designed for instant loading, responsive handling, and smooth 60 FPS performance across desktop and mobile browsers.

Manual Driving Tips

  • Starting smoothly: Hold Clutch (E), select 1st Gear (or R), apply gentle Gas (W), and release the clutch smoothly.
  • Avoiding stalls: Releasing the clutch at near-zero speed in a tall gear (2nd–4th), while holding the brake, or with zero throttle will bog down RPM and stall the engine.
  • Clean parking: Pull fully inside the painted lines, match the yellow ground arrow within ~12°, and come to a full stop.
